About us

Compassion, rooted in tradition

VKN Charities is a charitable organisation based in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, working since January 2020 for holistic health and the care of orphaned elders and children.

Our story

Why VKN Charities exists

VKN Charities was started with a simple belief: good health and a dignified life should be within everyone's reach. Since January 2020 we have worked across Coimbatore and Pollachi to promote holistic health through yoga, meditation, Ayurveda, Siddha and alternative therapies.

Alongside healing, we care for those society often forgets — elderly people left without family and children who have lost their parents — and support education so that young people can build a better future.
